Itinerary · 15 days / 14 nights · open-jaw

Osaka → Setouchi → Kyushu (One Piece) → Mt. Fuji → Tokyo In through Kansai, out through Tokyo. All 10 Straw Hat statues in Kumamoto Prefecture, the Sunny-go Train, a two-night Mount Fuji base, and Tokyo's One Piece flagships at the end.

Why this order: everything west happens first, then one flight puts you in Tokyo with Mount Fuji directly on the way in — Kawaguchiko → Hakone → Tokyo is a straight line east. No leg repeats, and you end at the airport with the best connection in Japan.

Day by day

Day1

Land at Kansai (KIX) → Osaka / Namba

  • Airport → Namba: Nankai express, ~40 min, ~¥1,500. Pick up an IC card (ICOCA/Suica) at the airport.
  • Check in around Namba / Shinsaibashi. First evening: Dotonbori — Glico sign, takoyaki, kushikatsu.
  • Arriving early? Namba Yasaka Shrine (the lion-head stage) is 10 minutes away.
Sleep: Osaka (Namba)
Day2

One PieceOsaka city — One Piece shopping + the classics

  • ONE PIECE 麦わらストア 梅田店 (Mugiwara Store Umeda), plus the Abeno branch if you want a second pass. Jump Shop also in Umeda.
  • Nipponbashi Den Den Town — Mandarake, Animate, K Books, gachapon floors.
  • Osaka Castle + park, Kuromon Market for lunch, Shinsekai / Tsutenkaku late afternoon.
  • Sunset: Umeda Sky Building Floating Garden Observatory.
Sleep: Osaka · Tip: keep the merch small here — the Kumamoto store has the pilgrimage-exclusive goods and you're carrying everything onward.
Day3

One PieceUniversal Studios Japan — One Piece Premier Summer

  • ONE PIECE Premier Summer 2026 runs 30 Jul – 19 Nov 2026: Premier Show 2026, the ONE PIECE × Story Ride (Elbaf), Sanji's Pirate Restaurant, Straw Hats Banquet Restaurant.
  • Show tickets are sold separately from the Studio Pass — book both, weekday if you can.
  • Full day: closed-toe shoes, at the gate 30 minutes early.
Outside 30 Jul–19 Nov: swap for a Kyoto day trip (Fushimi Inari at 7am → Kiyomizu → Gion) and keep Day 4 for Nara.
Day4

Kyoto or Nara — whichever you skipped

  • Kyoto: Fushimi Inari early, Higashiyama lanes, Kiyomizu-dera, Gion at dusk.
  • Nara: Todai-ji, deer park, Kasuga Taisha lantern path — 45 min from Osaka.
Sleep: Osaka · Prep: reserve shinkansen seats for tomorrow in the smartEX app tonight.

MoveHimeji → Hiroshima

Day6

Miyajima → evening shinkansen to Fukuoka

  • Ferry to Miyajima: Itsukushima torii, Daisho-in, Mt. Misen ropeway if the sky is clear.
  • Hiroshima → Hakata, ~1 h 5 min. Late dinner at the yatai stalls on the Naka River.
Sleep: Fukuoka (Hakata)
Day7

One PieceFukuoka morning → Kumamoto: Luffy + Chopper + the castle

  • Morning: Kushida Shrine, Canal City, Mugiwara Store 福岡店.
  • Shinkansen Hakata → Kumamoto, 35–45 min. Drop bags at the hotel or a station locker.
  • Statue 1 — Luffy: Prefectural Office promenade. Bus from Sakuramachi BT platform 25 (~20 min), or tram to Shiritsutaiikukan-mae + 10 min walk.
  • Statue 2 — Chopper: outside the main gate of Kumamoto City Zoo — free to see, no zoo ticket needed. Tram to Dobutsuen-iriguchi + 5 min.
  • Kumamoto Castle (reopened 13 Aug 2026) + Josaien castle town; free ONE PIECE 周遊ガイドブック at the Prefectural Office on a weekday.
  • Evening: Shimotori arcade, Kumamoto ramen, basashi. Mugiwara Store 熊本店 for the pilgrimage-exclusive goods.
Sleep: Kumamoto · Tomorrow: rental car from 08:00.
Day8

One PieceCar loop north: Sanji → Nami → Usopp → sleep in Aso

  • Statue 3 — Sanji: Mina Terrace (益城町交流情報センター), Kiyama 236, Mashiki. ~40 min from Kumamoto city.
  • Statue 4 — Nami: Moe-no-Sato / Mt. Tawara Exchange Center, Komori 2115-3, Nishihara — the windmill plain.
  • Statue 5 — Usopp: right in front of JR Aso Station. Lunch at the Aso roadside station next door (Aso dairy soft-serve).
  • Afternoon: Mt. Aso — Kusasenri plateau, Aso Volcano Museum. Crater access depends on the volcano alert level: check the same morning.
Sleep: Aso or Takamori · Driving: ~90 km, easy roads, one mountain pass.
Day9

One PieceCaldera south: Robin → Sunny-go Train + Franky → Kurokawa Onsen

  • Statue 6 — Robin: Kumamoto Earthquake Museum KIOKU, Minamiaso (former Tokai University campus). 9:00–17:00, closed Mondays. Go in — it's the museum that explains why these statues exist.
  • Statue 7 — Franky: Takamori Station. Ride the Minamiaso Railway Takamori ↔ Tateno on the Sunny-go Train (the Thousand Sunny–themed train) — reserved seat ¥510, or the open-air trolley car ¥1,010 (weekends/holidays, mid-March–November). Reserve online 30 days to 3 days ahead.
  • Evening: Kurokawa Onsen — riverside outdoor baths, ryokan, the wooden bath-pass ticket.
Sleep: Kurokawa Onsen · Note: the Minamiaso Railway is running normally after the July 2026 quake — reconfirm the day before.
Day10

One PieceLoop back south-west: Zoro → Brook → Jinbe → Kumamoto

  • Statue 8 — Zoro: Ozu Central Park, 5–10 min walk from Higo-Ozu Station, right on the way down from Aso.
  • Statue 9 — Brook: Fureai Hiroba, Mifune Town, beside the Dinosaur Park — you can see the giant dinosaur from the road.
  • Statue 10 — Jinbe: Sumiyoshi Kaigan Park, Uto — seaside, 5 min from JR Nagabeta, and one of the biggest statues. All ten done.
  • Return the car in Kumamoto by evening; celebratory Kumamoto ramen.
Sleep: Kumamoto · Route check: the local line between Uto and Yatsushiro was partly on substitute buses after the July 2026 quake. Nagabeta is north of Uto and fine, but verify.
Day11

MoveFujiKumamoto → flight to Haneda → bus to Kawaguchiko

  • Fly KMJ → HND, ~1 h 45, JAL/ANA/Solaseed, from about ¥14,000 one-way. Morning flights 07:35 / 08:10 / 08:40 / 10:25.
  • Haneda → Kawaguchiko by Keikyu limousine bus: ¥3,300, ~3 h, a few departures a day — book it in advance, it sells out. (Cheaper from Shinagawa at ¥2,300.)
  • Evening in Kawaguchiko: onsen, lakefront walk, first proper look at the mountain if the sky is clear.
Sleep: Kawaguchiko · Shinkansen alternative: Kumamoto → Mishima ~4 h 30, then Fujikyu bus ~1 h 45 — slower, but no airport transfer.
Day12

FujiMount Fuji day

  • Chureito Pagoda (Arakurayama Sengen Park) — the postcard shot: five-storey pagoda, Fujiyoshida town, Fuji behind. ~397 steps up; go early morning, the mountain hides behind cloud from late morning onwards. Fujikyu Railway to Shimoyoshida Station.
  • Oshino Hakkai — eight spring ponds in a village at Fuji's foot, thatched houses, clear water.
  • Lake Kawaguchi: Oishi Park (the classic lake + mountain frame), the lake cruise, Kawaguchiko Music Forest if you want the kitsch.
  • Autumn option: the Momiji Corridor maple tunnel (early–mid November).
Sleep: Kawaguchiko · Reality check: in summer Fuji is bare and hazy, in winter the air is clearest but the lake side is cold — the best odds of a clean view are Oct–Nov and April.
Day13

FujiKawaguchiko → Hakone → Tokyo

  • Cross to Hakone: Owakudani volcanic valley (black eggs, sulphur vents), Lake Ashi pirate ship, and the Hakone Shrine torii standing in the water at Motohakone.
  • Hakone → Shinjuku on the Romancecar, ~1 h 25, straight into Tokyo.
  • Evening: Shinjuku — Omoide Yokocho alleys, Kabukicho lights, or a quiet izakaya.
Sleep: Tokyo (Shinjuku) · Alt: camp an extra night in Hakone and drop Tokyo to one night.
Day14

One PieceTokyo — the One Piece day

  • ONE PIECE BASE SHOP — the official flagship, 4th floor of Shinjuku Marui Main Building, opened December 2025: Luffy Gear 5 limited figures, island-themed zones.
  • Entry is a lottery + advance time-slot reservation through the ONE PIECE BASE app — apply in the window a few weeks before you fly, not on the day. If you lose the lottery, check for leftover first-come slots.
  • ONE PIECE 麦わらストア 渋谷本店 (Mugiwara Store Shibuya flagship) for the widest general range.
  • Rest of the day: Shibuya Crossing + Miyashita Park, Akihabara for second-hand figures, Asakusa Senso-ji if you want the classic.
Sleep: Tokyo · Souvenir check: everything One Piece is cheaper to buy today than at the airport.

Fly out of Tokyo (HND or NRT)

The One Piece pilgrimage — all 10 statues

Built by the ONE PIECE Kumamoto Revival Project — Oda Eiichiro's hometown. Luffy went up in 2018 on the Prefectural Office promenade; the other nine followed across nine towns and villages between 2019 and 2022. All outdoors, all free, permanently accessible; only KIOKU and the zoo keep opening hours.

Mount Fuji — two ways

Option A — see it (works all year, this plan)

Two nights at Kawaguchiko, Fuji filling the end of the street. No permit, no altitude, no booking lottery. Works in every season, and it's what the Kawaguchiko + Hakone pairing in Days 11–13 is built for.

  • Best viewpoints: Chureito Pagoda, Oishi Park, Lake Kawaguchi north shore, Oshino Hakkai.
  • Go early: Fuji vanishes behind cloud most afternoons, especially in summer.
  • Cheap hop from Tokyo: Shinjuku → Kawaguchiko highway bus ¥2,200 (¥2,000 online), and it stops at 中央道下吉田 for Chureito Pagoda.

Option B — climb it (July 1 – Sept 10 only)

If your dates land inside the season, swap Day 12 for an overnight climb: bus from Kawaguchiko to the Yoshida 5th Station, hut stay, summit for sunrise, descend by mid-morning.

  • 2026 season: Yoshida + Subashiri trails open 1 July; Fujinomiya + Gotemba open 10 July; all four close 10 September.
  • Mandatory ¥4,000 fee on every trail, plus an online reservation that issues a QR wristband.
  • Trail gates shut from 2 pm to 3 am — start before 14:00 or after 03:00 unless you're staying in a mountain hut (booking a hut is the practical way).
  • Outside the season all trails to the summit are closed — no exceptions, and it's genuinely dangerous.

Logistics and money

Skip the 14-day JR Pass

At ¥80,000 it doesn't break even on this route — and once you fly Kumamoto→Tokyo, half the justification disappears. Instead:

  • IC card (ICOCA/Suica) for city trains, buses, trams, konbini.
  • Point-to-point shinkansen: Shin-Osaka→Kumamoto ¥18,960 (~3 h); Hakata→Kumamoto 35–45 min; Hakata→Hiroshima ¥8,570 unreserved.
  • SUNQ bus pass if you go bus-heavy in Kyushu: all-Kyushu 3 days ¥11,000 / 4 days ¥14,000; northern Kyushu 3 days ¥9,000.
  • Hakone Free Pass for the Day 13 loop (ropeway, pirate ship, buses).
  • If you still want all-you-can-ride: from 1 October 2026 the pass rises to ¥53,000 / ¥84,000 / ¥105,000 through agencies, but the official JR website keeps ¥50,000 / ¥80,000 / ¥100,000. Buy there, never through a reseller.

Kumamoto leg: car vs bus

Renting a car for Days 8–10 is what makes the statue loop pleasant — the Aso, Uto and Minamiaso statues have thin public transport. One-way drop-off in Kumamoto city is routine.

  • ~¥6,000–9,000/day plus expressway tolls; book an automatic and take the ETC card.
  • You need an International Driving Permit (1949 Geneva Convention format) plus your RU licence and passport. Get it in Russia before you fly — Japan cannot issue one for you.
  • Zoo parking ¥200; nearly every statue has free or cheap parking nearby.

Getting there from Russia

No direct Russia–Japan flights; connect via Istanbul, Dubai, Beijing/Shanghai or Seoul. Land at Kansai (KIX), leave from Haneda or Narita. Book it as an open-jaw ticket — two one-ways into KIX and out of Tokyo usually cost more than one multi-city booking.

Russian citizens need a visa — no visa-free entry, no visa on arrival. Apply at the Japanese Embassy in Moscow or a regional consulate with the day-by-day itinerary, all accommodation bookings and financial documents. Confirm current rules before paying for anything non-refundable.

When to go

Best overall: late October – mid November — Fuji's clearest viewing window, autumn maple at Chureito Pagoda, USJ One Piece running until 19 November, trolley train still running, comfortable walking weather.

Sakura run: late March – early April — Fuji + cherry blossom is the single best photo of the whole trip, but it's peak season: book Kawaguchiko 4–6 months out.

To climb Fuji: July 1 – September 10 only, and August is the most crowded and hottest for the Kyushu section. If climbing matters most, put Mount Fuji earlier in the trip and accept the summer heat in Kumamoto.

Two live conditions, checked 18 Sept 2026. (1) A second Kumamoto earthquake struck on 28 July 2026 (max intensity 7), worst along the Uto–Yatsushiro coast — the Aso side escaped largely intact. The Kyushu Shinkansen fully reopened on 18 September 2026, Kumamoto Castle reopened 13 August and the city zoo 4 August. The Uto–Yatsushiro local line is still partly on substitute buses; Nagabeta (Jinbe) is fine, but re-check each site before Day 10. (2) Japan announced a 「九州応援割」 Kyushu Support Discount — a national subsidy on Kyushu travel and accommodation, discussed at around 60% off for Kumamoto. If it's live when you book, your Kumamoto and Aso nights get much cheaper. Verify the launch rules before committing.
